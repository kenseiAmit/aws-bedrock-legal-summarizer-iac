COMMERCIAL SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE (SAAS) AND DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

This Commercial Software as a Service (SaaS) and Data Processing Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into as of October 1, 2026 ("Effective Date"), by and between:

OBJECTIONINSPECTION INC., a Delaware corporation having its principal place of business at 100 Innovation Way, Suite 400, San Francisco, CA 94105 ("Service Provider" or "Company"),
and
LEXGLOBAL PARTNERS LLP, a limited liability partnership organized under the laws of the State of New York, having its registered offices at 750 Lexington Avenue, 18th Floor, New York, NY 10022 ("Customer" or "Client").

WHEREAS, Service Provider provides proprietary artificial intelligence-assisted legal document review, analysis, and automated summarization platforms ("ObjectionInspection Platform"); and
WHEREAS, Customer desires to subscribe to and utilize the ObjectionInspection Platform to process, analyze, and generate executive summaries of corporate and litigation legal documents.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises herein contained, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND SUBSCRIPTION SERVICES
1.1 "Authorized Users" means Customer's designated partners, associates, and legal assistants authorized to access the SaaS Platform.
1.2 "Customer Data" means all electronic data, plaintext documents, briefs, and exhibits submitted by Customer to the S3 ingestion storage.
1.3 Service Scope: Service Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, worldwide subscription to access the ObjectionInspection cloud infrastructure, powered by foundational large language models hosted on Amazon Bedrock.

2. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA) AND UPTIME
2.1 Availability: Service Provider guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime for document processing APIs, excluding scheduled maintenance.
2.2 Processing Throughput: Summarization requests shall begin processing via automated event notifications within thirty (30) seconds of document upload confirmation.

3. FEES, BILLING, AND PAYMENT TERMS
3.1 Subscription Fee: Customer shall pay an annual base platform fee of $48,000, payable in advance in twelve equal monthly installments of $4,000.
3.2 Invoicing and Due Date: Invoices are issued on the 1st day of each calendar month and are due net thirty (30) days from invoice receipt.
3.3 Late Payment Penalties: Unpaid amounts after 30 days incur interest at the rate of 1.5% per month or the maximum statutory rate permitted by law.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY, DATA SECURITY, AND COMPLIANCE
4.1 Protection of Confidential Information: Each party agrees to hold all proprietary data in strict confidence and use no less than reasonable care.
4.2 Cloud Storage Security: All Customer Data stored in Amazon S3 buckets shall be protected with AES-256 server-side encryption and strict block public access controls.
4.3 AI Training Restriction: Service Provider warrants that Customer legal documents and generated summaries will NOT be used to train foundational AI models on Amazon Bedrock.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND OWNERSHIP
5.1 Customer Ownership: Customer retains exclusive ownership of all uploaded legal documents and all resulting AI-generated summaries and work products.
5.2 Provider Ownership: Service Provider retains all right, title, and interest in and to the ObjectionInspection platform, algorithms, and documentation.

6. TERM, TERMINATION, AND REMEDIES
6.1 Term: This Agreement is effective for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months, renewing automatically for successive one-year terms unless either party provides sixty (60) days written notice of non-renewal.
6.2 Termination for Cause: Either party may terminate immediately upon written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within thirty (30) days.
6.3 Post-Termination Data Retention: Upon termination, Service Provider shall retain Customer Data for thirty (30) days to permit retrieval, after which all data shall be purged securely in accordance with NIST 800-88 guidelines.

7. INDEMNIFICATION AND LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
7.1 Limitation: Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.
7.2 Aggregate Liability Cap: Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by Customer in the preceding twelve (12) month period.

8. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York. Any disputes arising hereunder shall be resolved through binding arbitration in New York City administered by JAMS.
